Research

Neural control of homeostasis

How the brain interprets vestibular and visceral information to coordinate autonomic, metabolic, behavioral, and immune responses.

01

Vestibular physiology and altered gravity

I study how vestibular circuits adapt to prolonged or repeated changes in gravitational load. My work aims to understand the neural mechanisms underlying vestibular compensation, sensory adaptation, and resilience, with relevance to both terrestrial physiology and spaceflight.

02

Thermoregulation and energy metabolism

My work examines neural pathways controlling body temperature and energy metabolism, particularly brown adipose tissue thermogenesis. Current projects focus on how peripheral signals engage hypothalamic oxytocin and vasopressin systems and sympathetic pathways to regulate thermogenic organs.

03

Gut–brain, vagal sensory, and neuroimmune signaling

I investigate how peripheral signals are detected by vagal sensory pathways and relayed to the brain to regulate feeding, metabolism, and inflammation. A major focus is on gut-derived hormones such as GLP-1 and CCK and their interactions with hypothalamic circuits, as well as vagal–central autonomic–sympathetic pathways that regulate peripheral immune responses.
