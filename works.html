Earlier presentations (before Kyoto Prefectural University)

The previous website archive includes presentations on heat acclimation, behavioral thermoregulation, thermal perception, hot-water bathing, and athletic training from 2016–2021. These are retained here as an earlier-career archive rather than mixed with the current research program.

  • 2021 — The 44th Annual Meeting of the Japan Neuroscience Society / 1st CJK International Meeting: Two-week heat exposure alters the behavioral response to heat and the possible involvement of thermo-TRP channels.
  • 2021 — The 98th Annual Meeting of the Physiological Society of Japan: Fundamental research on behavioral thermoregulation and thermal perception using a novel cross-shaped behavioral thermoregulatory system.
  • 2020 — The 8th International Conference on the Physiology and Pharmacology of Temperature Regulation: Thermal perception of heat may be suppressed during heat acclimation, which augments spontaneous exercise in mice.
  • 2018 — The 23rd Annual Congress of the European College of Sport Science: Evaluation of a novel short-term heat acclimation protocol using fan cooling.
