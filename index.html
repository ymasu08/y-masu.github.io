Physiology · Neuroscience · Autonomic Regulation

Yuta Masuda, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Department of Physiology, University of Fukui

I study how visceral and vestibular signals are integrated by the brain to regulate feeding, thermogenesis, inflammation, and adaptation to changing environments.

Explore research Publications researchmap

Research focus

From peripheral signals to whole-body physiology

Gut–brain signaling

How gut hormones such as GLP-1 and CCK engage vagal sensory pathways and hypothalamic circuits.

Thermoregulation

Neural control of brown adipose thermogenesis, body temperature, and physiological adaptation.

Vestibular physiology

Neural mechanisms that support vestibular compensation and adaptation to altered gravity.

Neuroimmune regulation

How autonomic and vagal pathways shape inflammatory responses in peripheral organs.
