Physiology · Neuroscience · Autonomic Regulation

Yuta Masuda, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor, Department of Physiology, University of Fukui

I study how vestibular and visceral signals are integrated by the brain to regulate adaptation to changing environments, thermogenesis, feeding, and inflammation.

Research focus

Neural mechanisms of adaptation and homeostasis

Vestibular physiology & altered gravity

Neural mechanisms underlying vestibular compensation, sensory adaptation, and resilience to altered gravitational environments.

Thermoregulation & energy metabolism

Neural control of body temperature and brown adipose thermogenesis through hypothalamic and sympathetic pathways.

Gut–brain, vagal sensory & neuroimmune signaling

How vagal sensory pathways link peripheral signals with brain circuits controlling feeding, metabolism, autonomic function, and inflammation.
